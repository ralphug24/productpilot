Elevate Consulting Research — Onboarding experience
Interview INT-005 · Participant 05
Research focus: confidence in providing personal information

[00:00] Interviewer: How did you feel about the information requested?
[00:17] Participant: I didn't understand why my date of birth was required so early. The page didn't explain how that information would be used or whether it would be shared with the clinic.
[01:06] Interviewer: Did that affect your progress?
[01:14] Participant: I hesitated to continue. I would feel more comfortable if the form explained the purpose of each sensitive field and who could see the information.
[02:02] Interviewer: What else was unclear?
[02:09] Participant: I couldn't tell whether insurance details were required to browse appointments. I wanted to check if my plan was accepted first. A clear required or optional label would help.
[03:10] Interviewer: Did you have trouble accessing the account?
[03:18] Participant: My password was rejected without explaining the requirements. I had to guess several times before it was accepted. The verification email arrived normally after that.
[04:01] Interviewer: What should the team prioritize?
[04:12] Participant: Clear explanations before asking for personal details. I can complete the form, but I need to understand what I am agreeing to and what I can leave until later.
