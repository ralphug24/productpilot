Elevate Consulting Research — Onboarding experience
Interview INT-003 · Participant 03
Research focus: understanding what is needed to book a first visit

[00:00] Interviewer: What brought you to the product?
[00:15] Participant: I wanted to see available appointments before deciding whether to register. I couldn't preview much until I finished setup.
[01:02] Interviewer: What felt unclear during setup?
[01:10] Participant: I wasn't sure which fields were required and which were optional. I stopped to look for details I didn't actually need to enter. Some healthcare terms on the welcome screen were unfamiliar.
[02:05] Interviewer: What happened after completing the form?
[02:13] Participant: I finished onboarding but didn't know what to do next. I expected a clear way to schedule my first visit. I also wasn't sure whether I needed a payment method before booking.
[03:01] Interviewer: Was account access straightforward?
[03:10] Participant: The verification code expired while I switched between my email app and the browser. I got a new code eventually, but the message didn't explain how long a code would be valid.
[04:00] Interviewer: What would you change first?
[04:08] Participant: Use plain language and make the next step obvious. Let me see enough of the booking experience to understand why I am entering all this information.
