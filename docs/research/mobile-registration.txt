Elevate Consulting Research — Onboarding experience
Interview INT-004 · Participant 04
Research focus: registration on a mobile device

[00:00] Interviewer: Walk me through registration on your phone.
[00:14] Participant: I entered my name and address first. Typing my insurance information was frustrating because I couldn't upload a photo of my card.
[01:04] Interviewer: Were you able to correct mistakes?
[01:13] Participant: I accidentally entered the wrong phone number. I couldn't find a way to edit it without restarting registration. I was worried I would lose the information I had already entered.
[02:06] Interviewer: What happened when you took a break?
[02:15] Participant: The page timed out while I looked for the card. My answers weren't saved, so I had to type them again. I wanted to pause and return when I had everything ready.
[03:01] Interviewer: How did appointment booking compare?
[03:10] Participant: It asked for my name and contact details again even though they were in my profile. I expected those details to be reused.
[04:08] Interviewer: What worked well?
[04:16] Participant: The text was easy to read and the buttons were large enough. The problem was repeating work and not being able to change an answer easily.
