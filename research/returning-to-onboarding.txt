Research focus: finishing an account across multiple sessions

[00:00] Interviewer: Tell me about finishing your account setup.

[01:05] Interviewer: What did you see when you returned?
[01:14] Participant: The page had timed out. I had to restart the form and repeat the same contact details. I couldn't see a record of what I had completed.
[02:04] Interviewer: How did you know when setup was finished?
[02:13] Participant: The progress indicator wasn't clear about the steps remaining. After the last screen, I wasn't sure whether the account was ready or whether I still needed to verify something.
[03:00] Interviewer: Were you able to update your preferences?

[04:01] Interviewer: What would help most?
