What do the RICE inputs mean?

Reach: estimated users affected per quarter. Impact: low (1), medium (2), high (3). Confidence: your confidence in the estimates, from 1–100%. Effort: estimated person-weeks. Score = reach × impact × confidence ÷ effort. Source counts are evidence coverage, not user reach.
