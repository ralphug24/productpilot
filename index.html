Score each opportunity you want to compare. A higher RICE score suggests more estimated benefit per unit of effort. You can choose another option and explain why. Unselected opportunities stay in the backlog.

RICE = Reach × Impact × (Confidence ÷ 100) ÷ Effort

Reach: users affected per quarter, 0 or more. Impact: 1 low, 2 medium, 3 high. Confidence: 1–100%, so enter 80 for 80%. Effort: total person-weeks, 0.1 or more.

Example: 1 × 3 × (2 ÷ 100) ÷ 3 = 0.02. Confidence of 2 means 2%, not a 1–3 rating. Source document counts are evidence coverage, not user reach. Reach and effort have no upper cap.
